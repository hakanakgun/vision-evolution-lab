# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Vision Evolution Lab aims to be a constructive, technically rigorous, and welcoming open-source project. Contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to treat each other with respect and to make room for different levels of experience and different viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

Examples include:

- giving specific, actionable, and respectful feedback;
- focusing criticism on code, evidence, design, or methodology rather than people;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- respecting privacy and avoiding publication of another person's private information;
- helping keep technical discussions reproducible and on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, sexualized conduct directed at others, sustained disruption, or publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, lock, or restrict contributions or participation that violate this policy. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and focused on protecting constructive participation.

## Reporting

Do not use a public issue for a sensitive conduct report. Contact the repository owner privately using a contact method published on their GitHub profile. Include relevant links or screenshots and enough context to evaluate the report.

This project policy is informed by the community-building guidance in Open Source Guides and the Contributor Covenant.
